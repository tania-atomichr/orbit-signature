Your email signature

Your name, title and logo are text and pictures on the email's own background, and each button is its own link.

    Then paste it into your email

    1. In Gmail, open Settings (the gear, top right), then See all settings.
    2. On General, scroll to Signature, press Create new, give it a name and paste into the box.
    3. Under Signature defaults, pick it for new emails and for replies.
    4. Scroll to the bottom and press Save Changes.
    1. In Outlook, open Settings (the gear), then Account, then Signatures.
    2. Press New signature, give it a name and paste into the box.
    3. Pick it for new messages and for replies, then press Save.
    1. In Mail on your Mac, open Mail, then Settings, then Signatures.
    2. Pick your account, press +, and untick Always match my default message font.
    3. Paste into the box on the right and close the window; it saves by itself.
